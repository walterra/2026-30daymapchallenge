Closer than it felt

Kyiv felt far away.
Lisboa is farther from home.

When Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, I was in Lisboa. Kyiv felt so far away. But measured from my home in Kössen, Tirol, Lisboa is the more distant city.

Lisboa Portugal

Kyiv Ukraine

1,356 km from Kössen

Lisboa is 647 km farther from Kössen than Kyiv.
Distance on a map and distance in the mind are not always the same.

Calculation, not an estimate. WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesics calculated with Python’s pyproj.Geod: Lisboa 2,002.934 km; Kyiv 1,356.105 km. Main labels rounded to the nearest kilometer. These are distances between published place reference coordinates, not a home address, road route or flight path.

Projection: ellipsoidal azimuthal equidistant, centered on Kössen. Distances and directions from Kössen are preserved; the coast is generalized. The map does not measure Lisboa–Kyiv distance or claim to quantify a feeling.
