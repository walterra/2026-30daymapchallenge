Read the gaps, not just the lines

The map shows operating downhill piste line features associated with Austria. Ski infrastructure follows the Alpine belt, especially in western and central Austria. Gold indicates a recorded snowmaking attribute, blue an explicit “no”, and pale gray an unknown value. Ski-area markers are optional.

Unknown does not mean no snow cannons. This is not a map of all snowmaking equipment, water consumption, or current snow conditions. Individual cannon locations are not included in this proof of concept: attempts to retrieve them through Overpass failed.

Counts represent mapped line segments, not unique named runs or kilometers. Polygon pistes and non-operating features are excluded. Cross-border features associated with Austria are not clipped. Named ski areas can overlap; they are not an official resort census.
