Teal circles show snowmaking reservoirs. Other facilities can be enabled as small ochre points; they are hidden initially to keep the overview legible. Dashed lines connect documented parent and child records. Choose a district, then a system, to inspect its local infrastructure. Only features matched by source ID to official GIS point or reservoir layers are mapped. Reservoir positions are derived polygon centroids; schematic circles are not their outlines. Unmatched records are not assigned invented locations.

Scope: the register’s Nutzwasser für Beschneiungsanlagen category, <alle aktiven> search status, all nine Tirol districts. Each returned enclosing report is expanded to include child records, which may have different statuses and functions. District filters describe the search/report association, not a new administrative-boundary survey.

Permissions are not measurements. Rates, annual limits, seasonal conditions and one-time filling rights cannot be added into actual water consumption. No cannon counts, pipeline routes, environmental-impact estimates, or live snow conditions are inferred.

Register status is not current permit clearance. The inspected Sölden example is marked “besteht” but includes permission deadlines ending in 2025. The expanded extract does not independently validate legal or operating status.

Source: Land Tirol · Wasserbuch / WIS. Each mapped facility links to its official source. Public inspection does not establish an open bulk-data license; reuse clearance remains unresolved. This is a research prototype, not a cleared open-data release.
