    Cities as attractors

    These are geographic bubble clusters, not country colours or statistical visitor segments. All source countries remain on the map when the source selection changes. The domestic-stay toggle hides every pair whose origin and destination countries match, not just the selected origin. Changing this filter loads a separately precomputed layout without the excluded bubbles; changing only the source keeps positions fixed. The size scale stays fixed in both cases. Each destination attracts its own origin bubbles, while a collision force separates neighbouring bubbles. Dense clusters can spill away from the exact city point.

    Coverage, method & sources

    This is a selected-destination PoC, not a complete ranking of Europe. TourMIS contains 154 reporting destinations in this extract, including Madeira and Tel Aviv. Blank cells are unknown, not zero. No missing counts are estimated.

    One bubble is drawn for every non-missing individual-country × destination count within the map frame. Continents, foreign/domestic totals and residual “Other” groups are excluded to avoid double counting. Reported zeros remain present at the minimum marker size; missing values are not invented. Domestic stays can be included by unchecking the filter. This view does not use the earlier eight-market statistical clusters.

    Both layouts are simulated once during preparation and stored as coordinates. Rendering, resizing and filtering never run a force simulation. Layout inspired by D3’s Clustered Bubbles: fixed city-coordinate attraction replaces free-moving group centroids, with D3 collision forces. The layout and area scale are independent of source selection; tiny values use a disclosed minimum radius. Hover or select a bubble to reveal a line to its true destination anchor. The selected-source list provides keyboard access.

    TourMIS “preferred definitions” mix hotels with all paid accommodation, and city with greater-city areas. Overlapping reporting areas must not be added together. Bednights measure person-nights, not unique travellers. Overseas markets remain valid origins; Madeira and Tel Aviv are reporting destinations in the extract.
