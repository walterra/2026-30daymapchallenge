    Shared visitor profiles

    Four exploratory clusters compare the shares of eight origin markets, not city size. Domestic stays are included in these fixed profiles, even when hidden by the foreign-destination filter. Cluster names show their two largest average shares, not exclusive visitor groups.

    Coverage, method & sources

    This is a selected-destination PoC, not a complete ranking of Europe. TourMIS contains 154 reporting destinations in this extract, including Madeira and Tel Aviv. Blank cells are unknown, not zero. No missing counts are estimated.

    Clusters use only the 103 destinations with counts for all eight markets: Netherlands, Germany, United Kingdom, France, Italy, Spain, United States and China. Shares are square-root transformed (Hellinger space), then grouped with deterministic four-means clustering. These are descriptive, experimental groups, not validated tourism segments. Unclustered destinations remain available in grey.

    TourMIS “preferred definitions” mix hotels with all paid accommodation, and city with greater-city areas. Overlapping reporting areas must not be added together. Bednights measure person-nights, not unique travellers. The outbound filter uses destination country; overseas markets are still valid origins.
