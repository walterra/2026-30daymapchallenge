THE RIDE / ground elevation
Kössen DorfGriesner Alm

From an open valley into the mountains.

A calculated 18.71 km cycling route begins at the same Kössen Dorf point as the Taubensee hike. It follows roads via Schwendt and Griesenau, then the asphalt Kaiserbachtal toll road to Griesner Alm. Visibility updates at roughly 150 m intervals. This is not a recorded ride or a traffic-free cycle trail: sections use roads with motor traffic.

Terrain only. Not a cycling guide.

20 m terrain grid, 32 km visibility search and a modeled eye height of 1.7 m. Trees, buildings, weather and Earth curvature are excluded; charcoal marks missing terrain. One OSM road-access exception is explicitly reviewed against the tourism board’s cycling description; see the method. *Visible area counts only modeled cells.
